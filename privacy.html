SipFit Privacy

SipFit is currently a static prototype. Bottle, sip, clean, weather, and preference data are stored locally in your browser storage.

Location and Weather

If you use location-based weather, your browser asks for permission. Weather is used only to adjust rinse and wash reminders. You can also enter a city manually.

No Account Required

The prototype does not require an account. Data is not synced across devices.

Delete Data

Clearing site data in your browser removes local SipFit records.

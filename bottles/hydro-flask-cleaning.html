Hydro Flask Cleaning Guide

Short Answer

Wash a Hydro Flask daily if used daily. Wash the same day after electrolytes, coffee, tea, sugary drinks, or protein. Deep clean flex straw lids, gaskets, and silicone parts weekly or sooner if odor appears.

Focus Areas

  • Flex straw lid
  • Lid gasket and silicone parts
  • Straw interior
  • Coffee and tea residue inside lid parts

Hydro Flask bottles are easy to use, but straw lids and silicone components need regular cleaning and full drying to prevent recurring smells.

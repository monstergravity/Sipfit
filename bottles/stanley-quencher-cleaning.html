Stanley Quencher Cleaning Guide

Short Answer

Wash a Stanley cup daily if used daily. Clean the straw and lid the same day after electrolytes, coffee, tea, sugary drinks, or protein. Deep clean the straw and lid weekly, or sooner if smell appears.

Focus Areas

  • Reusable straw interior
  • Rotating lid parts
  • Straw opening
  • Any silicone or gasket area

Straw cups are convenient, but straw systems create more hidden moisture areas than open-mouth bottles. Use a straw brush and let the lid dry fully.

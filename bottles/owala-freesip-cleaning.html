Owala FreeSip Cleaning Guide

Short Answer

Wash an Owala FreeSip daily if used daily. Wash the same day after electrolytes, coffee, tea, sugary drinks, or protein. Deep clean the straw, spout gasket, button area, and lid at least weekly or sooner if it smells.

Focus Areas

  • Straw interior
  • Spout gasket and rubber seal
  • Button and hinge area
  • Lid threading and mouthpiece

Why It Smells

The FreeSip lid has more hidden zones than a simple screw cap. Moisture and residue can stay in silicone and straw pathways even when the bottle body looks clean.

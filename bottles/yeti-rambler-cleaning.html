YETI Rambler Cleaning Guide

Short Answer

Wash a YETI Rambler daily if used daily. Wash the same day after coffee, electrolytes, sugary drinks, or protein. Focus on the chug cap, threading, and gasket because those parts hold residue and moisture.

Focus Areas

  • Chug cap and mouthpiece
  • Gasket or seal
  • Threading
  • Any lid part that stays damp

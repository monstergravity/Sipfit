The actual problem

Your bottle does not get dirty by the calendar.

It gets risky when residue, moisture, time, and warm weather stack together. The bottle body is easy. The lid, straw, gasket, mouthpiece, and threads are where taste and smell linger.

Electrolytes leave residue

Salt, flavor, and sweeteners can sit in the lid and straw long after the bottle looks empty.

Protein needs a quick rinse

Shakes and milk-based drinks should not sit warm in a sealed bottle or gym bag.

Coffee and tea linger

Oils and flavor carryover can stick around unless the cap, gasket, and threads get cleaned.

Should You Wash a Water Bottle After Electrolytes?

Short Answer

Yes. Wash the bottle the same day after electrolytes, especially if it sat warm or closed. Electrolytes are not the same as protein, but flavor, salt, and sweetener residue can linger in lids, straws, and gaskets.

What to Clean

  • Straw or spout
  • Lid and mouthpiece
  • Gasket or silicone seal
  • Threads and hidden crevices

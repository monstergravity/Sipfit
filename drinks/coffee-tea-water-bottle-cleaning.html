How to Clean a Water Bottle After Coffee or Tea

Short Answer

Wash the bottle the same day after coffee or tea. Coffee oils and tea residue can linger in stainless steel bottles, silicone seals, lids, and straws, especially if the bottle stays closed while damp.

Where Smell Hides

  • Silicone gaskets
  • Cap threading
  • Straw openings
  • Plastic lid interiors
  • Mouthpieces

Best Routine

Wash with warm soapy water, scrub the lid and gasket, rinse thoroughly, and let all parts dry open. If the coffee smell remains, deep clean the lid and consider replacing worn seals.
